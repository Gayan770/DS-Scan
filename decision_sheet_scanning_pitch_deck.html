The Operational Bottleneck

Understanding the Current Manual Workflow & Friction Points

Key Problem Flow

Decision sheets arrive with multiple nested attachments and embedded documents packed inside Word (.docx) files.

1 First, manually convert container Word doc into PDF.
2 Open & extract each embedded document one by one into PDFs.
3 Individually rename and upload each generated file to DMS.

Impact on Productivity & Efficiency

Takes Too Long

Up to 25 minutes spent manually clicking each document.

Wastes Team Time

Underwriters spend hours clicking and saving instead of real work.

Work Piles Up

Files get delayed quickly, especially during busy month-ends.

Risk of Mistakes

Repetitive manual clicks make it easy to miss attachments.
